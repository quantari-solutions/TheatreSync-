# Movie night — how to use TheatreSync

**What you get:** the movie on the projector screen, the sound in your headphones, and
your friend's sound in *their* headphones, all in time with the picture. The room
speakers stay quiet.

**What you need:** the Android phone with TheatreSync, the Chromecast switched on
(through the AV receiver to the projector), everyone on the same Wi-Fi, and a movie
**file** you own (MP4 works best). For Netflix and other streaming apps, see
[STREAMING-APPS.md](STREAMING-APPS.md).

## You (Android phone)

1. Connect your headphones to the phone.
2. Open **TheatreSync**.
3. **1 Choose a movie** → pick the file.
4. **2 Choose your TV** → tap your Chromecast.
5. **3 Start movie**. The picture appears on the screen and the sound comes through your headphones.
6. If the voices don't match the lips, tap **Sound is early** or **Sound is late** a few times. TheatreSync remembers this for next time.

Pause, skip and stop with the big buttons on the phone. You can lock the phone; the movie keeps going.

## Your friend (iPhone) — or you on the iPad

1. Connect headphones to the iPhone/iPad.
2. Open **TheatreSync** (installed through Swift Playgrounds, see [IOS-SWIFT-PLAYGROUNDS.md](IOS-SWIFT-PLAYGROUNDS.md)).
3. Under **Movies on your Wi-Fi**, tap the movie. (If nothing shows up, type the **Address** shown on the Android phone.)
4. Keep the TheatreSync screen open. Use **Sound is early/late** if needed — each person's setting is separate.

**Using the iPad yourself?** Start the movie on the Android phone, switch off
**Sound on this phone** there, and join from the iPad. The Android phone still has
to stay on, because it's sending the movie to the TV.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| Chromecast isn't listed | Switch the TV/projector on, check the phone is on the same Wi-Fi, wait 10 seconds |
| "The Chromecast couldn't play this file" | Use an MP4 file (H.264 video, AAC sound) |
| Room speakers still playing | Turn on **Mute the room speakers**, or turn the receiver down |
| Friend can't find the movie | Same Wi-Fi? Type the address shown on the phone |
| Sound drifts or stutters | Move closer to the Wi-Fi router; tell Claude the "Sync: … ms" number shown at the bottom |
